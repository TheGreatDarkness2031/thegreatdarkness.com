DATA HANDLING NOTICE

PUBLIC ACCESS TERMINAL // REVISED 10 NOV 2026
THE SHORT VERSION: This site uses no tracking cookies, no advertising and no analytics. It never asks for your name, email address or location. Anything you type into the Public Log Bulletin or a high-score board is posted publicly for everyone to see.

This notice describes how the website at thegreatdarkness.com actually handles information.

1. WHAT YOU TYPE

  • Public Log Bulletin (homepage): the handle and message you enter are saved with the time you posted, and shown publicly. Posts are checked by an automatic filter, and automated replies may answer them. Don't post your real name, address, phone number or anything else personal.
  • Arcade high scores: the name you enter and your score are saved and shown on the public leaderboards. A score that makes a leaderboard is also announced on the Public Log Bulletin (game, name and score).
  • NEXUS terminal: what you type stays in your browser. It isn't sent anywhere.

2. WHAT IS COUNTED

The homepage hit counter adds one to a running total each time the homepage loads. It keeps only the total, not who visited.

3. WHAT YOUR BROWSER REMEMBERS

The site uses no cookies. A few small settings are saved in your own browser's storage, never on our side:

  • whether you've already seen the connecting screen this session;
  • your DAY / NIGHT display choice on the log viewers;
  • your choices in the RSS channel viewer;
  • your personal best in some arcade games;
  • a short-lived copy of the latest status data, so pages load faster.

Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes all of it.

4. SERVICES THAT CARRY THE SIGNAL

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google (Apps Script and Sheets) provides the live data and stores the bulletin posts and high scores. A few images also load from Google.

Like any web server, these providers may automatically log technical details such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies. We don't receive or keep those logs. Live monitoring data comes from public sources (for example USGS, the U.S. Energy Information Administration and the National Weather Service) and is fetched by our servers, not by your browser.

5. LINKS THAT LEAVE THE NETWORK

The merch store (Printful), the support page (Ko-fi) and the outside news channels are run by other companies under their own privacy policies. Orders and payments are handled entirely by them: we never see your card or payment details.

6. CHILDREN

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them. Parents: the bulletin and score boards ask only for a handle, and kids shouldn't use their real names there.

7. REMOVAL REQUESTS AND CONTACT

To ask for a bulletin post or high score to be removed, or for any question about this notice, email thegreatdarkness2031@gmail.com. Tell us the handle and what it said (and, for a post, roughly when).

8. CHANGES

If this notice changes, the revision date at the top will change too.

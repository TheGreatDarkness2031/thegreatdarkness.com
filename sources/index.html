DATA SOURCES

PUBLIC ACCESS TERMINAL // REVISED 11 NOV 2026
NOT AN OFFICIAL ALERT SERVICE. The Great Darkness is an independent creative project. It combines real, public data with an original fictional story. The data below may be delayed, incomplete or wrong, and the status levels on this site (NOMINAL, WATCH, ELEVATED, CRITICAL) are its own, not official ratings. Do not use this site for safety decisions. For official alerts, contact your local authorities or the National Weather Service.

Every live reading on this site comes from one of the public sources below. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

LIVE DATA

U.S. GEOLOGICAL SURVEY // EARTHQUAKE HAZARDS PROGRAM

Earthquakes: Seismic monitor, Radar, Event Log, RSS Channel.
CREDIT: Earthquake data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.

U.S. GEOLOGICAL SURVEY // VOLCANO HAZARDS PROGRAM

Volcano alert levels: Volcanic monitor, Radar.
CREDIT: Volcano alert data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.

U.S. ENERGY INFORMATION ADMINISTRATION

Hourly electricity demand and forecasts: Power Grid monitor, Radar, Event Log.
CREDIT: Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration, Hourly Electric Grid Monitor (retrieved hourly; each reading shows its own date and time).

OPEN-METEO

Hourly temperatures for monitored cities: Temperature monitor, Radar.
CREDIT: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, licensed under CC BY 4.0. This site compares the readings with its own baselines; the deviations shown are its own calculation.

NOAA SPACE WEATHER PREDICTION CENTER

Space weather scales, Kp index and alerts: Space Weather monitor, Radar outline, RSS Channel.
CREDIT: Space weather data: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center.

NATIONAL WEATHER SERVICE

Active warnings: Severe Weather monitor, RSS Channel.
CREDIT: Warning data: National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). Shown for reference only; the levels on this site are its own, not official NWS products.

NATIONAL INTERAGENCY FIRE CENTER // WFIGS

Current wildfire incidents: Wildfire monitor, Radar.
CREDIT: Wildfire incident data: National Interagency Fire Center (WFIGS).

IODA // GEORGIA TECH INTERNET INTELLIGENCE LAB

U.S. internet outage alerts: Network monitor, Radar.
CREDIT: Internet outage data: IODA, Georgia Tech Internet Intelligence Lab.

U.S. COAST GUARD NAVIGATION CENTER

GPS satellite health (almanac): GPS / NAV monitor.
CREDIT: GPS almanac data: U.S. Coast Guard Navigation Center.

NASA/JPL CENTER FOR NEAR EARTH OBJECT STUDIES

Upcoming close approaches: Near-Earth monitor, Radar outline.
CREDIT: Close-approach data: NASA/JPL-Caltech CNEOS (SBDB Close-Approach Data API).

AI PROVIDER STATUS PAGES

Public service status of OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral and Cohere: AI Incidents monitor.
CREDIT: Status data from each provider's public status page: OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, Cohere.

NASA // NIST

News releases (NASA) and Cybersecurity Insights (NIST): RSS Channel.
CREDIT: Headlines link to the original articles at nasa.gov and nist.gov.

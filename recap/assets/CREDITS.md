- **Kokoro-82M** text-to-speech model and voice packs, by hexgrad.
  Apache License 2.0. https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
  ONNX copy used: onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX (same licence).
- **kokoro-js** (runs the model in Node.js), Apache License 2.0.
  https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro/tree/main/kokoro.js
- **transformers.js** (Hugging Face, Apache License 2.0) and
  **ONNX Runtime** (Microsoft, MIT): run the model.
- **phonemizer** (xenova, Apache License 2.0; text to phonemes, includes eSpeak NG compiled to
  WebAssembly). eSpeak NG is GPL-3.0; it is used as a tool to produce the
  audio and is not distributed with the videos or the website. GPL terms
  do not apply to the audio it helps produce.
- The narration is a synthetic voice. Every video description says so,
  and YouTube's altered or synthetic content setting is turned on.

## Data
All figures come from the monitors' recorded data. Sources and their
credit wording are the same as https://thegreatdarkness.com/sources/
(USGS, NWS, NOAA SWPC, EIA, NIFC, USGS Volcano Hazards, IODA, NASA/JPL,
CelesTrak/U.S. Coast Guard NAVCEN, NOAA NCEI, provider status pages).

## Music and sound
- **Low hum** under the voice: three sine tones (55, 110.3 and 164.9 Hz)
  generated by ffmpeg itself (aevalsrc) for each video, faded in and out.
  No recorded music or sound is used. Only sound generated with ffmpeg or
  CC0 material may be added, listed here with its source.

## Video tools
- **ffmpeg** (LGPL/GPL; used as a tool on the build machine, not
  distributed): joins the cards and the voice into the videos.
- **Playwright** (Microsoft, Apache License 2.0) and **Chromium**
  (BSD-style licence): draw the cards from /recap/render/.
- Cards, seal and lettering are the site's own. The typeface is the build
  machine's Courier-compatible monospace (Liberation Mono, SIL Open Font

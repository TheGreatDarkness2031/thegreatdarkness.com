SCENARIO 1 // TREELINE

You wake in leaf litter at the edge of a clearing in a hardwood forest, late October. A T-shirt, a hoodie, jeans, sneakers, a wool blanket. Nothing in your pockets. Someone will report you missing. Stay alive until they find you.

Time moves only when you act. When you leave, the world keeps going at a quarter of real time (an 8-hour day away is 2 hours out there) and you follow your standing order. Death is permanent.

This is a game, not survival training. The rules follow real outdoor and search-and-rescue guidance as closely as a game can, but do not rely on it in the field.

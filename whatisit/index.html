WHAT IS THE GREAT DARKNESS?

The Great Darkness is a warning.

July 1, 2031

On that day, mankind will shut all power off.

The lights will go out.

The screens will go dark.

The networks will fall silent.

For a while, the world will stop being connected.

And you should be ready.

WHY?

We live surrounded by electricity.

Lights come on with the flip of a switch.

Phones wake up when we touch them.

Televisions, computers, cars, appliances, networks and entire cities depend on power.

We've built a civilization that rarely has to experience true darkness anymore.

We've also built a civilization that rarely experiences true silence.

There is always something.

A notification.

A message.

A headline.

A video.

A conversation.

Another thing to click.

Another thing to watch.

Another thing demanding our attention.

Until there isn't.

JULY 1, 2031

On July 1st, 2031, we're going to shut it all off.

Not just your phone.

Not just your television.

Everything.

The lights.

The computers.

The internet.

The billboards.

The televisions.

The machines.

The noise.

The constant stream of information that we've convinced ourselves we can't live without.

For a moment, we're going to step away from the world we've built.

And we're going to see what remains.

WHAT WILL YOU DO?

That's the question.

When the lights go out, you won't be able to Google it.

You won't be able to ask an AI.

You won't be able to scroll through somebody else's experience.

You won't be able to watch a video about what it's like.

You'll have to experience it yourself.

Maybe you'll sit in the dark.

Maybe you'll light a candle.

Maybe you'll go outside.

Maybe you'll talk to your family.

Maybe you'll look at the stars.

Maybe you'll finally notice the person sitting next to you.

Maybe you'll discover that the world didn't disappear when the screens did.

THIS WEBSITE

The Great Darkness exists because the countdown has already begun.

This isn't a website telling you to hate technology.

Technology isn't the enemy.

Neither is the internet.

Neither is your phone.

The problem is forgetting that they're tools — and allowing them to become the world.

So explore.

Read the public log.

Leave something behind.

Play a few games in the arcade.

Look around.

There are things here that are meant to make you think.

And there are things here that are simply meant to be fun.

Because there's still time.

THE WARNING

JULY 1, 2031

Remember the date.

When that day comes, the world you've become accustomed to may suddenly become very quiet.

Don't panic.

Don't stare at a dead screen wondering what happened.

Look outside.

Look up.

Talk to somebody.

Listen.

For thousands of years, people looked into the darkness and saw the stars.

We built lights so we could see through the night.

We built machines so we could work through it.

We built networks so we could communicate across it.

We built screens so we could fill every empty moment.

And now...

we're going to turn it all off.
